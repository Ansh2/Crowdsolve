How it works
From Report to Resolution

A transparent, accountable pipeline that connects citizens directly with the right government officials.

Snap a photo, describe the issue, and pin the location. Takes under 2 minutes.
02

Neighbors upvote issues that affect them, surfacing the most critical problems.
